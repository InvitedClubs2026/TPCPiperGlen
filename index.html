A Letter to Our Members

A Club where championship golf and an elevated lifestyle come together. Where every generation finds something to love.

Dear Members,

There are places we visit. Places we enjoy. And then there are places that become part of our story.

For so many of us, TPC Piper Glen is that place.

It's where friendships begin on the first tee, where a quick lunch turns into an afternoon of laughter, and where some of our favorite memories are made with the people who matter most.

Now, imagine taking everything you love about Piper Glen and elevating it beyond anything you've experienced before.

That future is closer than you think.

Today, we are incredibly proud to announce a multimillion-dollar investment, marking one of the most exciting transformations in TPC Piper Glen's history.

A completely reimagined clubhouse. Extraordinary new dining and gathering spaces. An elevated fitness and recovery experience. More for our youngest Members. And meaningful improvements to the golf course that has always been at the heart of our Club.

But this transformation is about something much bigger than beautiful new spaces.

It's about creating a Club where your morning begins with possibility, your afternoon turns into another memory, and your family never wants the day to end.

The Piper Glen you love is becoming the Piper Glen you've always imagined.

And we cannot wait to show you what's coming.

More Than a Renovation. A Complete Reimagination.

Picture walking through the doors of a Club that feels entirely new, yet still has everything that makes Piper Glen so special.

Beautifully redesigned spaces. More energy. More opportunities to connect. Every detail thoughtfully considered to make the time you spend here even more extraordinary.

This is where the next chapter comes to life.
